# HR-ANALYTICS-DASHBOARD
- The HR Analytics Dashboard is a Power BI project that provides a clear overview of employee data, workforce demographics, and attrition trends.
- It helps HR teams monitor key metrics, identify factors affecting employee turnover, and make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention, workforce planning, and overall HR performance.

## Project Objective
- Develop an interactive Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard to monitor employee attrition, workforce demographics, and key HR metrics.
- The dashboard helps HR teams identify turnover trends, analyse employee characteristics, and support data-driven decisions to improve employee retention and workforce planning.

## Project Insights
- The organisation has 1,470 employees, with an attrition rate of 16.12% (237 employees left).
- Research & Development and Sales departments experience the highest attrition.
- Employees with 0–10 years of experience are more likely to leave the company.
- The 26–35 age group represents the largest portion of the workforce.
- Male employees make up 60% of the workforce, while 40% are female.
- Employees earning up to 5K have the highest attrition, indicating salary may influence employee turnover.
- Sales Executives, Research Scientists, and Laboratory Technicians are the largest job roles in the organisation.
- Interactive filters and visualisations enable HR managers to analyse trends by age, department, education, salary, and job role for better decision-making.

## KPI 
- Total Employees: The organisation has a total workforce of approximately 1,470 employees.
- Active Employees: Around 1,233 employees are currently active in the organisation.
- Attrition Count: A total of 237 employees have left the company.
- Attrition Rate: Approximately 16.12% of the workforce has experienced attrition.
- Average Experience: Employees have an average work experience of 7.01 years.
- Average Age: The average age of employees is 36.92 years, indicating a predominantly mid-career workforce.
